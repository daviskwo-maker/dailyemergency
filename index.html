Daily QR Registration and Emergency Muster

Emergency safeguard: This application supports personnel accounting. It does not replace evacuation, rescue, supervisor headcount or the approved emergency response procedure.

Scan Personnel QR

Enter the date, supervisor, shift and company once, then continuously scan personnel QR codes.

Accepted QR format: JSON such as {"WorkerID":"W001","WorkerName":"Ali","Company":"PEC","Trade":"Rigger"}, or pipe-separated: W001|Ali|PEC|Rigger.
